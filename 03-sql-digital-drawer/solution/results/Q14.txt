Q14. 확인: 14번 리뷰를 삭제한다 (DELETE, 삭제 전/후 개수 비교)

sql> SELECT COUNT(*) AS review_count_before FROM review;
| review_count_before |
|---------------------|
| 40                  |
(1 rows)

sql> DELETE FROM review
     WHERE id = 14;
(OK, 1 row(s) affected)

sql> SELECT COUNT(*) AS review_count_after FROM review;
| review_count_after |
|--------------------|
| 39                 |
(1 rows)
