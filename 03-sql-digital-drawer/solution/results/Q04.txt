Q04. 확인: 별점 5점 리뷰 중 가장 최근 것 5개 (WHERE + ORDER BY + LIMIT)

sql> SELECT id, artwork_id, rating, comment, created_at
     FROM review
     WHERE rating = 5
     ORDER BY created_at DESC
     LIMIT 5;
